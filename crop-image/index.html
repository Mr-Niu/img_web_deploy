What Does Cropping Mean?

Cropping removes the outer parts of a photo or graphic so you keep only the area you need. It is one of the fastest ways to improve composition, fit platform aspect ratios, or cut away distractions. Our tool runs entirely in your browser — upload JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG, or HEIC, drag the crop frame, choose a ratio like 1:1 or 16:9, apply Instagram or YouTube presets, then download in seconds.

Best Aspect Ratios for Social Media

Instagram posts use 1:1 (1080×1080 px); Stories, Reels, and TikTok use 9:16 (1080×1920 px). YouTube thumbnails are 16:9 (1280×720 px). Facebook cover photos are wide (820×312 px). X (Twitter) posts work well at 16:9 (1600×900 px). Pick a preset in the tool to lock the crop frame and export at the recommended pixel size.

Crop vs Resize

Crop changes which part of the image you keep — ideal for reframing or matching a platform ratio. Resize changes the pixel dimensions of the whole image without cutting content. Use crop first to frame the subject, then Resize Image if you need a different output width or height.

How to Crop Images Without Losing Quality

Use the largest source file you have. Avoid zooming the crop far beyond 100% if you want maximum sharpness. Export PNG for graphics with transparency, WebP or AVIF for photos on the web, and JPEG for universal sharing. The default quality of 90 is a solid balance between file size and clarity.
