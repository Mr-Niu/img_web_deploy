Frequently Asked Questions

Is FreeWebP really free?

Yes. FreeWebP is completely free with no signup, no subscription, and no hidden fees.

Do you store uploaded images?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your files never leave your device and are not stored on any server.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload <strong>JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG, and HEIC</strong> in the converter. Output can be WebP, AVIF, PNG, or JPEG. SVG and HEIC are decoded locally first.

Is there a file size limit?

Each file can be up to <strong>20 MB</strong>. Total batch size is limited to 200 MB.

Can I batch convert images?

Yes. Convert up to <strong>20 images</strong> at once and download them individually or as a ZIP.
